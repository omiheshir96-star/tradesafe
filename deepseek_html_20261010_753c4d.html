📚 Trading 101
1. What is Trading? ▼

Trading means buying and selling financial assets to make profit from price changes.

  • Forex: Currency pairs like EUR/JPY
  • Crypto: BTC, ETH, SOL
  • Commodities: Gold, Silver, Oil
2. Risk Management (MOST IMPORTANT) ▼
  • 1% Rule: Never risk more than 1% per trade.
  • Stop Loss: Always set it.
  • Risk:Reward: Risk $1 to make $2+.
3. Candlestick Charts ▼

Each candle has 4 prices: Open, High, Low, Close.

  • Green candle = price up
  • Red candle = price down
4. Technical Indicators ▼

EMA: Average price trend.

RSI: Above 70 = Overbought, Below 30 = Oversold.

5. Trading Psychology ▼
  • Fear: Closes winners too early.
  • Greed: Holds losers too long.
  • Revenge: Trying to recover fast.
  • FOMO: Entering late.
6. The Reality ▼
  • 80-90% of new traders lose money within 1 year.
  • Most "signal groups" are scams.
  • Real traders win 50-60%, manage risk well.
7. Golden Rules ▼
  • ✅ Only trade with money you can afford to lose.
  • ✅ Always use a stop loss.
  • ✅ Risk only 1-2% per trade.
  • ✅ Practice on demo first.
  • ❌ Never trade with emotions.
  • ❌ Never trust "guaranteed profit".
